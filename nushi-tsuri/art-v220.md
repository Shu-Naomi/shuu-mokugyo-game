# v220 fish original artwork

Built-in image generation created three original transparent atlases. The selected PNGs are copied without pixel edits. Each contains 25 yaw drawings and a breathing drawing; the shared Canvas renderer uses the actual silhouette bounds and lip coordinates. The loach selects its progressively frontal source cells before the face-on pose. Smelt and loach use matching mirrored right poses for the leftward turn: this removes backward-facing loach cells and keeps the smelt body position gradual across the front.

- `assets/fish-wakasagi-v220.png`: small silver smelt, dorsal and adipose fins.
- `assets/fish-dojo-v220.png`: cylindrical mud loach, short barbels, one dorsal and a rounded tail.
- `assets/fish-isaki-v220.png`: three longitudinal bands and a continuous spiny/soft dorsal fin.

Earlier trout-reference drafts were rejected because they transferred incorrect body/fin anatomy. The selected atlases were generated afresh without reference images, with `transparent_background: true`.

Biology references used for the descriptions and prompts:

- [琵琶湖博物館・ワカサギ](https://jmapps.ne.jp/ikimono/det.html?data_id=7965)
- [長野県水産試験場・ドジョウ](https://www.pref.nagano.lg.jp/suisan/joho/sakanatachi/dojo.html)
- [三重県水産研究所・三重大学、イサキの年齢査定 (2023)](https://www.museum.kagoshima-u.ac.jp/ichthy/INHFJ_2023_029_034.pdf)
